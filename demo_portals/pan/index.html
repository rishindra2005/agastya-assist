Important Instructions for Applicants / महत्वपूर्ण निर्देश

Form 49A is the statutory application form prescribed under Section 139A of the Income Tax Act, 1961 for allotment of a Permanent Account Number to Indian entities. Please review these essential guidelines:

  • The applicant must provide details in full uppercase English alphabets.
  • Aadhaar number must be quoted where available, and the name entered should match official Aadhaar records.
  • Upon initial registration, a temporary Token Number is generated and displayed. Note this number to resume application in case of session disconnection.
  • Providing an accurate mobile number and email ID is mandatory to receive digital dispatch of the e-PAN PDF.

Every applicant must submit or reference valid documentary proof under Rule 114 of Income Tax Rules:

  • Proof of Identity (POI): Aadhaar Card issued by UIDAI, Voter's Photo Identity Card, Passport, Driving License, or Ration Card having photograph of the applicant.
  • Proof of Address (POA): Aadhaar Card, Electricity Bill / Water Bill / Gas Connection Bill (not older than 3 months), Bank Account Statement, Passport, or Post Office Passbook.
  • Proof of Date of Birth (DOB): Aadhaar Card, Birth Certificate issued by Municipal Authority or Registrar, Matriculation / 10th Class Marksheet, or Passport.

The online PAN portal offers a fully digital, paperless application route via Aadhaar OTP authentication:

  • The demographic details (Name, Gender, Date of Birth, and Address) will be validated directly against the Aadhaar database.
  • An OTP will be transmitted to the mobile number registered in the Aadhaar profile.
  • No physical documents need to be signed or dispatched by courier when e-Sign authentication is successfully completed.
  • The photograph appearing on the Aadhaar card will be utilized on the physical PAN card.

The processing fee for Form 49A with physical delivery within India is ₹107.00:

  • Application Processing Fee: ₹93.00
  • Goods and Services Tax (GST @ 18%): ₹14.00
  • Total Amount Payable: ₹107.00 (Demo payment — no actual funds are charged or moved)
  • Supported payment options in the demo system include mock Net Banking, Debit/Credit Card, and UPI.

The Assessing Officer (AO) Code identifies the specific Income Tax Circle or Ward having territorial jurisdiction over the applicant:

  • AO Code consists of 4 parts: Area Code (3 letters), AO Type (1-2 letters), Range Code (1-3 digits), and AO Number (1-3 digits).
  • Individual salary earners and residential applicants select their AO code based on their place of residence.
  • Business applicants, professionals, and corporate bodies select their AO code based on the principal place of business.
  • Use the interactive AO Code Search Tool embedded within Form 49A Tab 2 to search your city and select your ward automatically.
  • DO: Provide father's full name even for married female applicants, unless mother is a single parent.
  • DO: Verify the spelling of the applicant's name against Aadhaar records to prevent mismatch rejections.
  • DO NOT: Use abbreviations or initials unless the official name in records contains initials.
  • DO NOT: Submit multiple applications; possessing more than one PAN is illegal under Section 272B of Income Tax Act.
