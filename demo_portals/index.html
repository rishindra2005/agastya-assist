⚠️ Fictional Demonstration Notice & Disclaimer

These portals are fictional, standalone demonstration replicas designed solely for the Agastya Assist assistive AI hackathon showcase. They are NOT official government portals, do not submit or store real data, and do not connect to any official government department, UIDAI, Income Tax Department, Election Commission, or payment gateway.

All numbers, tokens, OTPs, and acknowledgement receipts generated within these replicas are entirely simulated and fake. Do not enter real personal, confidential, or sensitive financial information.
