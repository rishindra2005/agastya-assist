SPICe+ (Part A) - Name Reservation

SPICe+ (Simplified Proforma for Incorporating Company Electronically Plus) Part A is the statutory web-form for reserving a name for new company incorporation or changing an existing company's name.

  • Select company type, class, category, and sub-category via dependent dropdowns.
  • Look up 2-digit National Industrial Classification (NIC) division code.
  • Propose up to two names with instant automated availability check.
  • Submit for name reservation with instant demo SRN generation.
