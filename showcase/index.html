Agastya Assist · अगस्त्य सहायक

Speak what you need. Agastya fills the government form.

Government portals have to be complete and informative, so they will never be simple. Agastya makes them simple to use: you talk in your own language, it reads the portal, asks only what it doesn't know, fills the page, and hands control back to you for the captcha, OTP, consent and final submit.

Your language

It listens and answers in 11 Indian languages

Speech is recognised and spoken with Sarvam AI. Agastya detects the language you speak and replies in it, in its own script, so the voice sounds natural.

The problem

Portals must explain everything, so they are hard to use

A portal has to carry every rule, exception and legal note, which is why simplifying the page itself rarely works. Here is how much a citizen meets in the demo replicas we built to mirror four common flows.

How it works

One request, five steps

  1. You speak

    Tap the mic and say what you need, in any of the 11 languages.

  2. It understands

    Picks the right service and checks your saved profile and documents.

  3. It asks less

    Only missing details, two or three related ones per question.

  4. It fills the page

    Works on the real portal page while you watch it live.

  5. You finish

    Captcha, OTP, consent and Submit stay with you, inside the same screen.

Recorded demos

Four flows, recorded end to end

Real voice, real agent, on clearly labelled demo replicas of the portals. The citizen's steps are done through Agastya's live view.

Desktop and phone

The same assistant on a laptop or a phone

The web app installs to the home screen as a phone app. Voice, questions, the live view and taking control all work by touch, with a type box for OTPs.

You stay in charge

Three locks between the agent and the Submit button

Action guard

Every click is checked against final actions in English and Hindi: submit, pay, e-verify, OTP, login, captcha. Payment gateways are off limits.

Tool gate

Every browser tool call passes an allow-list. Anything not explicitly approved, such as running page scripts, is refused.

In-page lock

While the agent works, final buttons and form submits are disabled inside the page itself. When you take control, the lock lifts.

Always yours: passwords, OTPs, captchas, consent and declaration boxes, payment and the final submit. Sensitive numbers like Aadhaar and PAN are masked on screen and in speech, and your profile stays on your own device.

Under the hood

Built with

  • Sarvam AI speech recognition and speech synthesis (saaras:v4, bulbul:v3)
  • Claude agent with portal playbooks and tool use
  • Playwright browser with a live screencast over Chrome DevTools
  • Browser MCP for reading complex page structure
  • FastAPI with WebSockets for voice and live control
  • Form 16 reader that fills the income-tax schedules
  • Local SQLite profile with validation and masking
  • Installable web app (PWA) for phones
The portal screens in the demos are fictional replicas built for this hackathon. Each one carries a "DEMO REPLICA — NOT A GOVERNMENT WEBSITE" banner, and nothing was submitted to any government service. Agastya Assist is a prototype for the Vikas hackathon.
